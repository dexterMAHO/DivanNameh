{% comment %} A miniature medallion: the ornamental mark for a section or an empty state. Drawn inline rather than through the icon sprite on purpose. A element builds a shadow tree, and class selectors from the page do not reach inside it, so a mark needing several different fills (paper, ink, gold) cannot be styled from a . The icon sprite still works because an icon inherits one fill and one stroke from its host. Medallions appear a handful of times per page, so inlining them costs little; long verse listings use no medallions.
